---

## 2. v1 means all of this

1. A limited company owns the product and the domain.
2. `t4.ie` serves this app over HTTPS with settled privacy + terms.
3. A company can save work across devices (done).
4. Every scheme has a human re-read of the official URL the week you launch.
5. Knockouts stay conservative and tested (done for current rules).
6. AI drafts are labelled, rate-limited, never submitted as the application (done).
7. You can restore a user’s data if a laptop dies (DB on deploy).
8. You would demo this to a LEO without apologising for stale rates.
